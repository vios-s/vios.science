Yuyang Xue is a Postdoctoral Research Associate in the School of Engineering at the University of Edinburgh, working within the VIOS group as part of the CHAI AI Hub, under the supervision of Prof. Sotirios Tsaftaris. His research interests span multi-agent AI systems, generative models, computer vision, trustworthy and robust machine learning, with applications across medical imaging and the general computer vision.

He completed his PhD in Computer Science at the University of Edinburgh (School of Engineering). He holds an MSc in Computer Science from the University of Southampton and a BSc in Computer Science from Fuzhou University, China.
