## Why Join VIOS
VIOS brings together researchers working across machine learning, computer vision, causality, healthcare, agriculture, and scientific infrastructure. We value strong technical foundations, intellectual curiosity, and an interest in interdisciplinary work.

There are several reasons applicants choose to work with us:

1. **Ambitious, method-driven research**  
We work on core AI questions and apply them to consequential problems in medicine, agriculture, and the life sciences.

2. **Interdisciplinary collaboration**  
Our projects span academia, healthcare, and industry, including collaborations across the University of Edinburgh, the University of Nottingham, Canon Medical, CHAI, and other partners.

3. **A supportive research environment**  
We aim high, but we also invest in mentoring, collaboration, and careful research practice.

4. **Strong career outcomes**  
Our alumni have gone on to roles in academia, startups, and industry, including positions at Google, Apple, FAIR, CMU, Ultromics, TU Darmstadt, and other institutions.

If you are interested in joining as a PhD student, postdoctoral researcher, visitor, or collaborator, please read the guidance below before contacting us.

## Important information for PhD students applying
* If we have open positions, we welcome applications from outstanding and ambitious students with strong potential for research.
* Successful applicants come from a range of backgrounds, including engineering, computer science, mathematics, physics, and related fields. We value diversity in background, perspective, and experience, and we strongly encourage applications from groups under-represented in academia.  
* Being fluent in both spoken and written English is essential. We accept the following English language qualifications at the minimum grades specified:
    * IELTS Academic: total 6.5 with at least 6.0 in each component
    * TOEFL-iBT: total 92 with at least 20 in each section
    * PTE Academic: total 61 with at least 56 in each of the Communicative Skills scores
    * CAE and CPE: total 176 with at least 169 in each paper
    * Trinity ISE: ISE II with distinctions in all four components
* Your English language qualification must be no more than three and a half years old from the start date of the programme, unless you are using IELTS, TOEFL, PTE Academic, or Trinity ISE, in which case it must be no more than two years old.
* **Making informal enquiries informative**: applications for open positions must be submitted through the relevant University system. Informal enquiries about research fit can be sent by email to Prof. Tsaftaris or the relevant supervisor. Please state the relevant opportunity clearly in the subject line, confirm that you have read this page, and include specific questions. Questions on the application process and scholarships should be directed to the Postgraduate Office of the School of Engineering: [EngGradOffice@ed.ac.uk](mailto:EngGradOffice@ed.ac.uk). It is strongly recommended that you attach a CV and include details of your qualifications, academic history, notable achievements, ranking within your cohort if available, and links to any publications with a note on your contribution.
* **Self-funded students**: we occasionally accept strong self-funded candidates, preferably through scholarships. If this applies to you, please state it clearly in the subject line of your enquiry. 

## Visiting students
* We welcome visiting students and researchers from around the world. Visits are subject to University approval, visa requirements, and available capacity, so we are only able to host a limited number. If you would like to enquire about a visit, state this clearly in the subject line, attach a CV, and include your proposed duration, possible start date, and the research direction you are interested in pursuing. 

## Internships
* At present, we do not accept applications for paid internships from outside the UK. We may, however, host paid interns within the University or, in some cases, within the UK.
