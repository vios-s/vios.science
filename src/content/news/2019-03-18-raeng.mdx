Sotos was appointed [Canon Medical/Royal Academy of Engineering Research Chair in
Healthcare
AI](https://www.raeng.org.uk/news/news-releases/2019/march/academy-announces-2019-research-chairs-and-senior).
Over five years, this endowed position supports research into key
challenges in developing and deploying AI in healthcare. The position is
made possible through the support of Canon Medical Research Europe, the
Royal Academy of Engineering, and the School of Engineering.
